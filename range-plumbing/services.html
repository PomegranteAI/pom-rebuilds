Service

Oil boiler servicing

Regular servicing keeps an oil boiler running at its most efficient, cutting fuel bills and avoiding costly breakdowns. We clean the heat exchanger and check nozzles, photocells and electrodes, the parts that wear and cause soot build-up or random shutdowns.

  • Recommended at least once a year
  • Heat exchanger cleaning
  • Nozzle, photocell and electrode checks
  • Lower fuel bills

Service

AGA servicing

Oil-fired AGA cookers should be serviced at least every six months. Low-sulphur fuel means more carbon build-up, which is usually why an AGA loses temperature. Our engineer completed product training at the AGA factory in Shropshire.

  • Six-monthly servicing
  • Carbon build-up and temperature loss
  • Maintenance and repair
  • Factory-trained engineer

Call 07785 310400

Service

Worcester Greenstar baffle removal

Worcester Greenstar Camray condensing boilers can suffer from baffles that are very hard to remove when servicing has been missed, and many engineers will not touch them. We offer a baffle-removal service, priced on the condition of the boiler and its service history.

  • Advanced fault finding training
  • Sulphur build-up removed
  • Priced on boiler condition
  • Back to a regular service plan
