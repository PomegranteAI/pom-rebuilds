About Range Heating

A personal, specialist oil heating service from Great Notley, near Braintree.

Our story

Trained by the manufacturers

Range Heating is an OFTEC registered business. The owner first trained with a company specialising in AGA servicing and completed product training at the AGA factory in Shropshire. He was also a domestic boiler engineer with Heateam, the service division of Baxi/Potterton.

Installer training has been completed at leading oil boiler manufacturers including Grant, Worcester-Bosch, Potterton and HRM Wallstar, plus the Riello service engineers course. As oil is mostly used in outlying rural areas, we cover a wide part of Essex.
